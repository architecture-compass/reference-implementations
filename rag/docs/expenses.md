## Home office equipment

Employees may expense home office equipment such as a desk, chair, monitor or headset, up to $300 per calendar year. Purchases require your manager's approval before you buy.

## Travel meals

While traveling on company business, meals are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Receipts and deadlines

Submit expenses in the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Itemized receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card from finance. Corporate cards must never be used for personal purchases.
