## Merit increase budget

Each manager receives a merit increase budget equal to 4% of their team's total base salary. Individual increases can range from 0% to 8% as long as the team total stays within budget.

## Promotion increases

Promotions come with a base salary increase of between 8% and 12%, depending on the size of the step between levels.

## Deadlines

Merit and promotion recommendations must be entered in the HR portal by March 15. Changes take effect on April 1.

## Confidentiality

Compensation recommendations are confidential. Do not share individual numbers with anyone outside HR and your own management chain.
