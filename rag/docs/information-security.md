## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required on every company system, including email, the HR portal and source code repositories.

## Reporting incidents

Report any suspected security incident, such as a phishing email, a lost laptop or an exposed password, to the security team within one hour. Use the #security-incidents channel or email security@northwind.example.

## Customer data

Customer data must stay in approved company systems. Never send customer data to personal email accounts or upload it to unapproved AI tools.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.
