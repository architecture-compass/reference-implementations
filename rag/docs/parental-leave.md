## Who is eligible

All full-time and part-time employees of Northwind Lending are eligible for parental leave after completing 90 days of employment. Contractors and interns are not eligible.

## How much leave you get

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Part-time employees receive the same 16 weeks, paid at their regular part-time rate.

## When to take it

Parental leave can be taken in one continuous block or in up to three separate blocks. All leave must be used within 12 months of the birth, adoption or placement.

## How to request it

Tell your manager and submit a request in the HR portal at least 30 days before your leave starts, or as soon as possible if the date is not known in advance. HR confirms your dates and pay within five working days.
