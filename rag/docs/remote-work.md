## Remote days

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on their shared office days with their manager.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 US Central time, wherever they are working.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to two weeks per calendar year. Requests must be approved by your manager and by the legal team at least 15 days before travel.

## Equipment and security

Company laptops must be used for all work. Never work on customer data over public Wi-Fi without the company VPN.
